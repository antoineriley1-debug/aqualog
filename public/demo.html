Falsification Detection

4It quietly flags readings worth a second look

When one shift's readings come in suspiciously perfect and unchanging while other shifts vary normally, FacilityH2O raises a soft advisory — a private nudge to spot-check, never an accusation.

facilityh2o.com/facility/northgate
⚑ Reading consistency check — worth a second look
3rd Shift: the last 3 entries are near-identical and near-perfect (pH 9.50, 9.50, 9.50), while other shifts those same days averaged pH 8.9. Gap of 0.6 between this shift and the others.
Advisory pattern only — not proof of anything. Recommend spot-verifying these readings against the logbook or a re-test.
  • Catches the pattern auditors look for: one shift always "perfect" while reality varies across the others.
  • Visible only to administrators — operators never see it, so it never feels accusatory on the floor.
  • Stays silent on naturally varying data, so when it speaks, it's worth your attention.
