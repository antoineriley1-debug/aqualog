## 1. Purpose

This Data Retention Policy establishes standards for how long data collected by FacilityH2O is retained, how it is backed up, and how it is securely disposed of when no longer needed or upon system decommissioning. These standards are designed to meet regulatory requirements under ASHRAE Standard 188, The Joint Commission (TJC) Environment of Care and Infection Control standards, CMS Conditions of Participation, and applicable state healthcare facility regulations.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all data stored within the FacilityH2O system, including data generated by users at any of the FacilityH2O Inc. facilities served by FacilityH2O.

---

## 3. Data Types and Retention Periods

### 3.1 Water Chemistry Readings

**Description:** Test results logged by users, including pH, free/total chlorine, temperature, conductivity, oxidation-reduction potential (ORP), heterotrophic plate counts (HPC), Legionella results, and other water quality parameters.

**Retention Period:** Minimum **24 months (2 years)** from the date of entry.

**Regulatory Basis:**
- ASHRAE Standard 188-2018 requires water management program records to be retained for a minimum of 1 year; 2 years is the FacilityH2O and FacilityH2O Inc. standard to provide a fuller compliance record.
- The Joint Commission (Environment of Care Standard EC.02.05.01) requires documented evidence of water management program activities.
- CMS Conditions of Participation recommend water management documentation be available for survey.

> **Note:** In practice, all chemistry readings will be retained indefinitely as long as the system is active, given their low storage footprint and high compliance value. The 24-month figure is the minimum guaranteed retention, not a deletion schedule.

### 3.2 Corrective Action Records

**Description:** Notes, actions taken, and follow-up documentation linked to out-of-range readings.

**Retention Period:** Minimum **24 months** from date of entry.

**Regulatory Basis:** Same as Section 3.1. Corrective action records are considered part of the water management program documentation.

### 3.3 Alert Records

**Description:** System-generated alerts triggered by out-of-range readings, including alert timestamp, threshold breached, user notified, and alert acknowledgment status.

**Retention Period:** Minimum **24 months** from alert generation date.

### 3.4 User Account Records

**Description:** User names, email addresses, roles, assigned facilities, account creation dates, and last login timestamps.

**Retention Period:** Retained for the duration of employment/engagement. Deactivated accounts are retained for **12 months** after deactivation for audit trail purposes, then deleted.

**Exception:** If an account is associated with a security incident or investigation, the record is retained until the investigation is closed plus 3 years.

### 3.5 System Activity Logs

**Description:** Login/logout records, page access logs, data modification logs, and export logs.

**Retention Period:** Minimum **12 months** from event date.

**Purpose:** Security monitoring, audit trail, compliance verification.

### 3.6 Backup Archives

**Description:** Encrypted database backups.

**Retention Period:** Rolling **90-day** backup retention. Individual backups are overwritten after 90 days unless flagged for preservation due to a pending audit or investigation.

---

## 4. PHI Disclaimer

FacilityH2O does **not** store Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined under HIPAA. The system records water chemistry data for building infrastructure — not patient information, clinical data, or any data that identifies or relates to individual patients.

Users are prohibited from entering PHI or any patient-related data into FacilityH2O. If PHI is inadvertently entered, it will be identified and deleted upon discovery by the system administrator.

## 5. Backup Frequency and Procedures

| Backup Type | Frequency | Retention | Storage |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Full database backup | Daily (automated) | 90 days | Supabase encrypted storage |
| Incremental backup | Every 6 hours | 7 days | Supabase encrypted storage |
| Off-site snapshot | Weekly | 30 days | Separate encrypted cloud region |

**Backup Verification:** Backup integrity is verified monthly by restoring a test backup to a staging environment.

**Encryption:** All backups are encrypted using AES-256 before storage. Encryption keys are managed separately from the backup data.

**Access:** Backup access is limited to the system administrator (Antoine Riley) and authorized FacilityH2O IT personnel.

## 6. Data Deletion Procedures

### 6.1 Routine Deletion

Data that has exceeded its retention period is eligible for deletion. Routine deletion is performed quarterly by the system administrator with a deletion log maintained for audit purposes.

### 6.2 User Account Deletion

When a user account is deactivated:
1. The account is disabled (login prevented)
2. The account record is flagged as inactive
3. After 12 months, the account record and associated PII (name, email) are deleted
4. Water chemistry records entered by the user are retained (de-identified by removing the direct link to the specific user) for the full chemistry data retention period

### 6.3 Facility Offboarding

When a FacilityH2O Inc. facility is removed from FacilityH2O:
1. Data is placed in a read-only archive state
2. A full data export is provided to the FacilityH2O facility manager and FacilityH2O Inc. facility leadership
3. Data is retained in archive for 12 months following offboarding
4. After 12 months, facility data is permanently deleted with a signed deletion certificate

## 7. System Decommission

In the event FacilityH2O is decommissioned entirely:

### 7.1 Notice
All facility stakeholders (FacilityH2O management, FacilityH2O Inc. leadership) will receive minimum **90 days' advance notice** of decommission.

### 7.2 Data Export
- A full export of all chemistry readings, corrective actions, and alert records (CSV and PDF) will be provided to each facility before shutdown
- Exports will be organized by facility, covering all data within the retention window

### 7.3 Data Deletion
Following confirmation of successful export:
1. Application data is deleted from production databases
2. Backup files are securely wiped
3. A **Certificate of Data Destruction** is issued to FacilityH2O and FacilityH2O Inc.
4. Hosting provider accounts are closed

### 7.4 Regulatory Obligations
Exported data must be retained by the receiving organization (FacilityH2O Inc. / FacilityH2O) per their own document retention policies and applicable regulatory requirements. FacilityH2O's deletion does not relieve the organization of its compliance documentation obligations.

---

## 8. Legal Holds

If FacilityH2O data is subject to a legal hold, litigation, government investigation, or regulatory audit:
- Automatic deletion of the affected data is suspended immediately
- Affected data is flagged and preserved until the hold is lifted in writing
- The system administrator will work with FacilityH2O and FacilityH2O Inc. legal counsel to comply with all preservation requirements
