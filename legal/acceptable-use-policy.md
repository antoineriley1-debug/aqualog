# FacilityH2O Acceptable Use Policy

**System:** FacilityH2O — FacilityH2O Inc. Water Chemistry Tracking Portal
**Owner:** Antoine Riley / FacilityH2O
**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026
**Last Updated:** April 2, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs the authorized use of FacilityH2O, an internal water chemistry logging and compliance tracking portal operated by Antoine Riley on behalf of FacilityH2O and deployed for use within the FacilityH2O Inc. hospital system. All users of FacilityH2O are required to read, understand, and comply with this policy.

---

## 2. Authorized Users

Access to FacilityH2O is restricted to:

- **FacilityH2O employees** assigned to FacilityH2O Inc. facilities
- **FacilityH2O Inc. employees** who have been granted access by an authorized FacilityH2O administrator
- Contractors explicitly authorized in writing by Antoine Riley or a designated FacilityH2O/FacilityH2O Inc. manager

**Access is site-specific.** Users are authorized to access data only for the facility or facilities to which they are assigned. No user (other than system administrators) is authorized to view, access, or modify data belonging to other FacilityH2O Inc. facilities.

Access credentials are individual and non-transferable. Users may not share login credentials with anyone, including colleagues, supervisors, or contractors, regardless of circumstance.

---

## 3. Permitted Uses

FacilityH2O may be used only for the following authorized purposes:

- Logging water chemistry test results (pH, chlorine, temperature, conductivity, biological results, etc.)
- Recording corrective actions taken following out-of-range results
- Viewing compliance dashboards and trend reports for your assigned facility
- Generating compliance documentation required by ASHRAE 188, The Joint Commission, or CMS
- Receiving and reviewing automated alerts for out-of-range readings
- Managing user accounts (administrators only)

FacilityH2O is a **facility infrastructure management tool only.** It is not authorized for any clinical, patient-care, or administrative healthcare function outside of water management.

---

## 4. Prohibited Actions

The following actions are strictly prohibited and may result in immediate access revocation and disciplinary action:

### 4.1 Credential and Access Violations
- Sharing your username or password with any other person
- Using another user's credentials to access the system
- Accessing the accounts or data of other users without authorization
- Attempting to access facility data outside your assigned facility or facilities
- Using automated scripts or bots to access the system

### 4.2 Data Export and External Sharing
- Exporting, copying, or transmitting FacilityH2O data to unauthorized external systems, personal email addresses, personal devices, or third-party cloud services without written authorization from FacilityH2O management
- Downloading bulk data exports except for documented compliance reporting purposes
- Sharing system screenshots, reports, or data with individuals outside FacilityH2O Inc. or FacilityH2O without authorization
- Posting FacilityH2O data on social media or public platforms of any kind

### 4.3 System Integrity
- Attempting to probe, scan, or exploit vulnerabilities in FacilityH2O or its underlying infrastructure
- Injecting malicious code, scripts, or commands into the system
- Deliberately entering false, misleading, or fabricated chemistry readings
- Altering, deleting, or tampering with historical records without documented authorization
- Attempting to bypass authentication, access controls, or audit logging

### 4.4 Unauthorized Use
- Using FacilityH2O for personal purposes unrelated to water chemistry compliance
- Using FacilityH2O to store, process, or transmit Protected Health Information (PHI) of any kind
- Accessing FacilityH2O from outside FacilityH2O Inc./FacilityH2O managed networks without approval (VPN required for remote access)

---

## 5. Monitoring Notice

**All activity on FacilityH2O is logged and monitored.**

By using FacilityH2O, you consent to monitoring of your activity, including but not limited to:
- Login and logout timestamps and IP addresses
- Pages accessed and data viewed
- Records created, modified, or deleted
- Export and download actions
- Failed login attempts

These logs are retained for a minimum of 12 months and may be reviewed by Antoine Riley (system administrator) and authorized FacilityH2O management for security, compliance, and audit purposes.

Users have no expectation of privacy with respect to their use of FacilityH2O. This is a professional tool for facility compliance — all activity is subject to review.

---

## 6. Violations and Consequences

Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy may result in:

### 6.1 Immediate Actions
- Immediate suspension or revocation of FacilityH2O access
- Escalation to FacilityH2O management and/or FacilityH2O Inc. leadership
- Preservation of system logs as evidence

### 6.2 Disciplinary Actions
Depending on the severity and nature of the violation:
- Formal written warning
- Suspension without pay (subject to HR determination)
- Termination of employment or contractor relationship
- Referral to law enforcement (in cases of data theft, fraud, or criminal conduct)

### 6.3 Legal Consequences
Serious violations involving unauthorized data access, export of facility data, or intentional system damage may be subject to civil or criminal penalties under applicable federal and state laws, including the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (18 U.S.C. § 1030).
