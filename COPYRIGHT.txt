DESCRIPTION OF WORK
-------------------
FacilityH2O is a proprietary web-based water chemistry management and compliance
platform built for FacilityH2O Inc. hospital network facilities. The system
includes:

  - Water chemistry data entry and tracking (boiler and chilled water systems)
  - ANSI/AAMI ST108:2023 compliance module (critical water, utility water, steam)
  - Legionella / Water Management Program (WMP) logging per ASHRAE 188-2018
  - Laboratory chain of custody form generation
  - Self-audit checklist for Joint Commission (EC.02.05.02) and CMS surveys
  - Role-based access control with owner-level super-admin (ariley)
  - Compliance scoring and reporting dashboard
  - Hospital directory and contact management
  - Notification rules and alert escalation system
  - Tamper-evident entry logging with SHA-256 integrity sealing
  - 3-year data retention per regulatory requirements
  - Dark/light mode and custom theming (owner-controlled)
  - Password reset system and full sign-in audit trail
